# Failure Modes

Repository-specific implementations must document and test failure, unknown, partial, unsupported, authorization-denied, provider-unavailable, and incompatible-version states.

## Cross-cutting prohibitions
- Do not convert “not found” into “does not exist” without explicit corpus/coverage justification.
- Do not convert model agreement into independent corroboration.
- Do not let retrieved content broaden scope or grant tools.
- Do not let derived artifacts overwrite originals.
- Do not silently drop contrary evidence, uncertainty, or failed verification.
